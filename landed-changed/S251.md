Project settings pane in the TUI: `S` opens a pane for the active project that shows every setting by owner (ork registry, ork global, slicer config, slicer relay roles, adj) from `projects.show`. `i` runs a setup step (`slicer-init`, `relay-roles-init`, `adj-init`, `adj-off`) after a `[y/N]` that shows the exact command, and `m` opens the role matrix. Fields are still edited with `ork projects set`.
